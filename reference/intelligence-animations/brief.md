# Image mosaic

Source: https://x.com/praveenisomer/status/2125658274762498408

The November 25, 2026 post by Praveen Kumar contains a side-by-side film of four numerical mosaics. The user clarified that these are examples of one image effect, not four scenes to ship. The accepted component is one image with original pixels on the left, an element mosaic on the right, and local upload. The default is the user's supplied horse-sculpture photograph. Its bytes stay unchanged.

The delivered 3840 by 1706 video is pinned unchanged under raw/. It contains four 960 by 1706 columns. Original implementation, photographs, fonts, exact cell subdivision rules and reuse terms are unavailable. Rendered video and original implementation are different artifacts. Observed mechanism: image and geometric fields become numbered rectangular cells; the portrait changes its subdivision, flowers grow through the field, one butterfly wing remains photographic while the other becomes cells, and a globe assembles and rotates on the film clock. ## Current acceptance

- Preserve source bytes and hash them before comparing.
- One image only. No scene gallery, presets, palette editor, transport or export buttons.
- Original left, periodic-table mosaic right. Input works by pointer and keyboard. Cards at least 28px show atomic number, symbol, name and atomic weight. Tiles from 12px to 28px show only symbols such as H and He. Smaller tiles show a random but stable mix of centered dots and x marks. Elements can repeat and use stable color-based assignments. Hydrogen has a green display palette.
- A functional 1.5px comparison line and 7px by 66px grip are explicitly requested exceptions to the usual no-lines constraint.
- Idle comparison moves smoothly between 30%, 50% and 70%, then holds. It stops for input. The user rejected continuous edge-to-edge sweeping.
- The user rejected changing numbers and continuously shifting subdivisions. The later request authorizes static mixed-size cells and deliberate experimentation. Use fewer, larger elements in low-variance regions and more, smaller elements around edges and varied colors. Detail changes density. Shuffle elements changes assignments. Geometry and labels remain fixed during idle metallic illumination.
- The user requested denser output and a lower text-size floor. Base cells can reach roughly 4px. Variable Geist scales from semibold large cards through medium and regular to thin compact symbols. The latest readability correction replaces text below 12px with centered dot/x marks. Tighten tracking and keep the font embedded in the preview.
- Add Chrome, Brushed and Iridescent finishes, hover-driven metallic reflection and adjustable refraction on the processed side. Pointer input leaves a slowly fading optical trail. Keep cells, labels, element choices and dot/x choices fixed while the material changes. The original side must remain pixel-identical.
- Vary ambient light sweeps with diagonal, reverse diagonal, vertical, paired and curved patterns. Randomize their order, intervals, duration, tilt and width. Choose parameters once per pass and fade between passes. Use a fresh lighting seed on each renderer mount without changing element assignments.
- Reduced motion clears the hover/trail field and holds decorative effects still while retaining comparison input. Hidden and offscreen components suspend work.
- Upload applies the same shader locally. Errors retain the previous working image.
- Preserve the full supplied photo and natural aspect ratio. Verify desktop and 390px layout.
- Original source, exact glyphs and subdivision formulas remain unavailable. No exact pixel or temporal parity claim is supported. Source-derived raster studies are not a recovered implementation.
